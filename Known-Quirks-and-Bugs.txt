Various Known Quirks & Bugs

Irritating Maze - can't pull up Unibios In Game Menu with ABC+Start
only with Start+Select (possibly because IM only has two buttons?)

King of the Monsters - If PICKnMIX is active and the user advances to 
KotM while the board has hard drip #7 ON (Free Play), it will not 
automatically advance until the user selects a different game.

Real Bout 2 - shows the Giga Power splash screen, no other titles
show the Neo Geo splash screen when cycling attract modes

Thrash Rally - takes about two seconds longer than any other title to load in

Zupapa - Unibios In Game Menu is visually corrupted.  It still works 
and the Cheat Database (first menu option) is still accessible and works.
